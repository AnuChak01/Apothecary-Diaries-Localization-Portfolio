Why This Page Exists

This page explores how cultural symbolism can be localized without removing its original context. Each flower was researched not only as a botanical element, but as a narrative device contributing to characterization, foreshadowing, and thematic storytelling within Apothecary Diaries.

Page 03 Dialogues 対話
Localization Decisions
  • 01
    EN / JP Toggle

    The most direct localization demonstration on the page. Switching the entire scene in either language, not word for word rather as per the cultural and tonality. Each version was written to stand independently in its own linguistic and cultural context.

  • 02
    Honorific Retention — さま · 妃

    In the Imperial court, how a character is addressed encodes rank, distance, and deference. Replacing these with generic English equivalents flattens the hierarchy carried in every spoken line.

  • 03
    Stage Directions in Red

    Narrative action cues rendered in crimson, keeping the reader inside the moment without needing illustration.

  • 04
    Inner Monologue Framing

    Maomao's internal voice is styled at reduced opacity and lighter weight, visually separating her observations from the spoken dialogue around her.

  • 05
    Translation Annotations

    Notes embedded in the English version for terms where cultural context cannot be carried by translation alone.

Why This Page Exists

A localization portfolio is best demonstrated through practice. This scene has been provided as a sample localization, preserving not just meaning but tonality, hierarchy, and voice. It was chosen deliberately: it captures Imperial court behavior, Maomao's dry wit and clinical reading of a situation others misread entirely, and a solution that arrives too late, ultimately changing her fate completely. The dark blue of this page was chosen to match the scene's weight.
