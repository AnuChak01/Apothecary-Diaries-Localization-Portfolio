Apothecary Diaries

薬屋のひとりごと

概要 — The Story

Apothecary Diaries:
A Tale of Herbs and Hidden Truths

原作Natsu Hyuga
ジャンルHistorical  |  Mystery
設定Imperial China

Set in a historical period based on Imperial China, Maomao — a young woman trained under her veteran apothecarist father — grew up among herbs, remedies and one truth: 知識は命を救う — Knowledge saves lives. The series follows her journey from being kidnapped and sold to the Imperial palace as a servant, where she one day came across a case that turns her fate. Inside the Imperial palace she meets different people, challenges and grabs the attention of an influential eunuch, who holds a special identity.

薬屋（くすりや） — literally "apothecary" — an archaic term used for a medical professional who formulates, dispenses, and sells medicines, herbs, and remedies. Here, in Apothecary Diaries, it carries a dual meaning of someone who understands that every substance has two faces: the one that heals, and the one that harms.

重要キャラクター  — Key Characters
猫猫
Poison Lover  ·  Healer
M  A  O  M  A  O  ·  猫猫
“知らない方がいいこともある......” Some things are best left unknown....

She is the main protagonist in Apothecary Diaries novel and series written by Hyuga Natsu. A young woman with eccentric curiosity, highly intellectual and down-to-earth characteristics. Possessing expert knowledge in herbs and medicine, a 毒愛好家 · poison-lover and the go-to-person for solving cases of the Imperial Palace.

壬氏
Imperial Court Eunuch  ·  Moon Prince
J  I  N  S  H  I  ·  壬氏
“美しさというものは、時に呪いにもなる...” Beauty, at times, can become a curse....

Jinshi understands something most beautiful people never learn — that 美しさ · Beauty is not a gift freely given. It is a carefully maintained performance, a veil that reveals and conceals depending on who is watching. As a high-ranking eunuch of the Inner Court, his true identity is known only to a privileged few — making every smile a negotiation between what is shown and what is meant.

羅門
Master Physician  ·  Adopted Father
L  U  O  M  E  N  ·  羅門
“推測でものを言うな。” Don't speak based on conjecture.

A veteran apothecary of quiet brilliance, a former inner court physician. He is Maomao's adoptive father and mentor, who raised her not with rules but with 知恵 · Wisdom, laying before her everything he knew about herbs, poisons and the thin line between them. He never taught Maomao what to think. Only how to observe. That single act shaped everything that follows.

玉葉
Jade Consort  ·  Maomao's Patron
G  Y  O  K  U  Y  O  U  ·  玉葉
“美しさというものは、時に呪いにもなる...” In this court, even a smile can be a blade.

The direct mistress of Maomao within the Inner Palace. 玉葉妃 · the Jade Consort carries her rank with effortless grace. Kind on the surface, sharp and observant underneath. Her foreign heritage gives her an air of brilliance that stands apart. She recognised Maomao's talent before anyone else did. Gyokuyou and Jinshi share a complex alliance of mutual respect, hidden agendas and one shared goal: protecting what matters most.

紫水
Maomao's Confidante  ·  Insect Lover
S  H  I  S  U  I  ·  紫水
“虫は冬を越せない。ただ、子供だけを残して。” Insects never survive the winter. They only leave behind their children.

Like 水 · Water — she takes the shape of whatever space she enters. On the surface, a maid servant with a cheerful smile and an unusual fondness for insects. Bright, cheerful and deliberately easy to underestimate — noticed only by those paying attention. Similar to Maomao, she speaks the same quiet language that goes unnoticed until it is already too late.

羅漢
Military Strategist  ·  Shogi Master
L  A  K  A  N  ·  羅漢
“私には、誰の顔も木の駒のように見える。” To me, everyone's face looks like a wooden game piece.

A military strategist of extraordinary talent and deeply peculiar character. He holds immense power within the court, yet suffers from a rare condition — unable to recognize human faces, seeing every person as a wooden game piece. To him, life is a game of 将棋 · Shogi. Every conversation a move. Every silence a strategy. Genius, tactician and as sly as a fox — his connection to Maomao runs deeper than she ever expected.

将棋（しょぎ） — a traditional Japanese strategy board game, played with 20 pieces per player on a 9×9 grid. Comparable to chess in depth and prestige — every piece has a purpose, and sacrifice is part of the strategy.
